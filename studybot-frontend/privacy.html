Privacy Policy

Draft — to be reviewed before public launch.

What we store

Your email, name, a hashed password, and the documents you upload (the original file and the text extracted from it).

Third-party services that process your data

Jina AI (embeddings). When you upload a document, the extracted text is sent to Jina AI in pieces so it can be converted into searchable vectors. Each question you ask is also sent to Jina AI for the same purpose.

Groq (answer generation). When you ask a question, your question and the most relevant excerpts from your selected documents (with their filenames) are sent to Groq, which generates the answer.

Your email address, name and password are not sent to either service.

Your control

You can move documents to the recycle bin and delete them permanently, or delete your account, which removes your documents and stored data.
